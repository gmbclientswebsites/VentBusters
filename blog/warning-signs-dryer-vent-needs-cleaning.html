5 Warning Signs Your Dryer Vent Needs Cleaning

A clogged dryer vent isn't just an inconvenience — it's one of the more common contributing factors in home clothes-dryer fires, per U.S. Fire Administration data. The good news is your dryer usually gives you warning signs long before things get dangerous. Here are the five to watch for.

1. Clothes Take More Than One Cycle to Dry

This is almost always the first sign homeowners notice. When your vent is restricted, hot, moist air can't escape efficiently, so your dryer has to run longer — sometimes two or three cycles — just to get clothes dry.

2. The Dryer or Laundry Room Feels Unusually Hot

A properly venting dryer releases heat outside your home. If your laundry room feels noticeably hotter than usual, or the dryer itself is hot to the touch on the outside, trapped heat from a blocked vent is a likely cause.

3. You Notice a Burning or Musty Smell

A faint burning smell when the dryer runs can mean lint is overheating inside the vent — a serious fire warning sign that shouldn't be ignored. A musty smell can indicate trapped moisture and mold growth inside the duct.

4. Visible Lint Around the Exterior Vent Cover

Step outside and look at your dryer's exterior vent cap. If you see lint collecting around the flap, or the flap doesn't open freely when the dryer runs, airflow is already restricted.

5. It's Been Over a Year Since Your Last Cleaning

Even without obvious symptoms, the U.S. Fire Administration recommends cleaning your dryer vent at least once a year — more often if you do laundry daily, have pets, or have a long duct run between the dryer and the exterior wall.

If two or more of these sound familiar, it's time for a professional inspection — not a DIY fix. A full vent cleaning removes lint that's often impossible to reach with a household brush alone.

What to Do Next

The fix is usually quick: a standard dryer vent cleaning takes just 30–45 minutes and helps restore proper airflow, which can reduce fire risk and drying time. If you're not sure whether your vent needs service, a free airflow & safety check can confirm it.
