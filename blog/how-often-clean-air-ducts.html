How Often Should You Clean Your Air Ducts?

"How often should I clean my air ducts?" is a question homeowners ask often — and the honest answer is: there's no fixed schedule that fits every home. Here's how to think about it.

There's No Universal Interval

The EPA doesn't recommend routine air duct cleaning on a fixed calendar. Instead, it points to specific conditions that suggest cleaning may be worth considering — visible mold growth, vermin infestation, or excessive dust and debris actually being released from your registers. Air duct cleaning is not currently a service we offer (it's coming soon), but the same visible-evidence approach applies whenever you do have it done.

Situations Worth a Closer Look

  • Pets in the home — pet dander and fur can accumulate in return vents and ductwork faster than in pet-free homes.
  • Recent renovation or construction — drywall dust and debris can get pulled into the system.
  • Visible mold or musty odors — especially common in humid climates where condensation collects inside ductwork. We don't identify or remediate mold; if you see visible growth, have it evaluated by an appropriately licensed mold professional.
  • New home purchase — you don't know the previous owner's maintenance habits, so a baseline inspection can be useful.

Signs It's Time, Regardless of the Calendar

Beyond a fixed schedule, watch for visible dust blowing out of vents, a musty smell when the HVAC kicks on, inconsistent airflow between rooms, or a noticeable uptick in dust on furniture even after cleaning.

What a Cleaning Actually Involves

A professional air duct cleaning uses HEPA-filtered vacuum equipment to extract buildup from the entire system — supply and return vents included — so debris is removed, not just stirred up and redistributed. A full home typically takes 2–4 hours depending on system size.

The best way to know for sure? A quick visual inspection. It's free, takes about 20 minutes, and tells you exactly what's happening inside your system.

Bottom Line

If it's been more than three years, you have pets or allergy sufferers at home, or you're just not sure — it's worth having a technician take a look. Book a free inspection and we'll give you an honest answer, not a sales pitch.
