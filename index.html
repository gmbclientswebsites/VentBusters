Don't Ignore The Warning Signs

Signs Your Vents May Need Attention

Your dryer vent and HVAC duct system can collect lint, dust and debris over time. Here are some common signs that it may be time to have your system evaluated.

👕

Clothes Taking Longer to Dry

Restricted dryer airflow is a common cause of longer drying cycles.

🌬️

Weak Dryer Vent Airflow

Weak airflow at the exterior dryer vent may indicate lint buildup or another obstruction.

🧶

Excess Lint Around the Dryer

Lint around the dryer or vent connection may indicate leakage, damage or restricted airflow.

💨

Visible Dust Around Registers

Noticeable buildup around supply registers or return grilles may be a reason to have the accessible duct system evaluated.

🔍

Debris Inside Accessible Ducts

Visible dust or debris inside accessible duct openings may indicate that cleaning should be considered.

👃

Unusual Odors From the HVAC System

Persistent odors coming from the ventilation system should be evaluated to identify the source before deciding on the appropriate service.

Dryer safety: If you smell burning while your dryer is running, stop using the dryer and have the appliance and vent system evaluated. If there is smoke or an active fire, leave the home and call 911.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost? +

Pricing depends on vent length, routing, and level of buildup in your home. There's no one-size-fits-all number — we assess your system in person and give every customer an exact, no-surprise, no-obligation price after a free on-site visit.

How often should I clean my dryer vent? +

The U.S. Fire Administration recommends cleaning dryer vent ductwork every year. Some homes may need more frequent service depending on dryer usage, vent length, the number of bends, and how quickly lint accumulates.

How long does a dryer vent cleaning take? +

A standard dryer vent cleaning takes about 30–45 minutes, depending on vent length and accessibility.

What are the warning signs I need dryer vent cleaning? +

Common warning signs include clothes taking longer than usual to dry, a burning smell when the dryer runs, excess lint around the dryer or vent connections, and weak airflow at the exterior vent flap.

Do you offer same-day or emergency service? +

Yes. Same-day and next-day appointments may be available for urgent dryer vent concerns. If you see smoke, flames, or an active fire, leave the home and call 911.

Is Vent Busters insured? +

Yes, Vent Busters is insured. Every technician is trained and follows our documented cleaning and safety process on every visit.
