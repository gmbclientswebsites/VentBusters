Frequently Asked Questions

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost? +

Pricing depends on vent length, routing, and level of buildup in your home. There's no one-size-fits-all number — we assess your system in person and give every customer an exact, no-surprise, no-obligation price after a free on-site visit. Air duct cleaning is coming soon and not yet available to book.

How often should I clean my dryer vent? +

The U.S. Fire Administration recommends cleaning your dryer vent at least once a year. Homes with long duct runs, pets, or daily laundry loads should be cleaned every 6–9 months to reduce fire risk and keep drying times fast.

How long does a duct or dryer vent cleaning take? +

A standard dryer vent cleaning takes 30–45 minutes. A full home air duct cleaning typically takes 2–4 hours depending on the number of vents and system complexity.

What are the warning signs I need duct or vent cleaning? +

Common warning signs include clothes taking longer than usual to dry, a burning smell when the dryer runs, visible dust around vent covers, musty odors when the HVAC turns on, and higher-than-normal energy bills.

Do you offer same-day or emergency service? +

Yes. We keep same-day and next-day appointments open for dryer vent emergencies and fire-risk situations. Call our team and we'll get a technician out as fast as possible.

Is Vent Busters insured? +

Yes, Vent Busters is insured. Every technician is trained and follows our documented cleaning and safety process on every visit.
