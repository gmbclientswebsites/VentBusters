Don't Ignore The Warning Signs

Is Your Home At Risk?

If any of these signs sound familiar, your dryer vent may need professional attention.

👕

Clothes Take Forever to Dry

Restricted airflow is a common cause of longer drying times.

🔥

Burning Smell While Drying

Stop the dryer and disconnect it if it's safe to do so. A burning smell requires prompt evaluation and may involve the appliance, electrical components, or the vent system.

💨

Excess Lint Around the Dryer

Lint collecting behind the dryer or around vent connections may indicate leakage, damage, or restricted airflow.

🌬️

Weak Exterior Airflow

If the exterior vent flap barely opens while the dryer is running, the exhaust pathway may be restricted.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost? +

Pricing depends on vent length, routing, and level of buildup in your home. There's no one-size-fits-all number — we assess your system in person and give every customer an exact, no-surprise, no-obligation price after a free on-site visit. Air duct cleaning is coming soon and not yet available to book.

How often should I clean my dryer vent? +

The U.S. Fire Administration recommends cleaning dryer vent ductwork every year. Some homes may need more frequent service depending on dryer usage, vent length, the number of bends, and how quickly lint accumulates.

How long does a dryer vent cleaning take? +

A standard dryer vent cleaning takes about 30–45 minutes, depending on vent length and accessibility.

What are the warning signs I need dryer vent cleaning? +

Common warning signs include clothes taking longer than usual to dry, a burning smell when the dryer runs, excess lint around the dryer or vent connections, and weak airflow at the exterior vent flap.

Do you offer same-day or emergency service? +

Yes. Same-day and next-day appointments may be available for urgent dryer vent concerns. If you see smoke, flames, or an active fire, leave the home and call 911.

Is Vent Busters insured? +

Yes, Vent Busters is insured. Every technician is trained and follows our documented cleaning and safety process on every visit.
