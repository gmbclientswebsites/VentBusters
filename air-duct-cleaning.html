Why It Matters

Cleaner Ducts, Less Buildup

Your air ducts circulate the same air through your home hundreds of times a week. Over time, accessible components can collect dust, pet dander, and pollen. Our air duct cleaning removes accumulated debris from accessible components when an inspection indicates cleaning is appropriate.

  • Inspection of accessible supply and return vents
  • HEPA-filtered vacuum extraction of accessible components
  • If we observe material that may require mold assessment, we'll recommend evaluation by an appropriately licensed professional
  • Before/after photo documentation when conditions allow

Air Duct Cleaning FAQ

Is air duct cleaning available now? +

Not yet — air duct cleaning is coming soon. Our dryer vent cleaning and airflow & safety check services are live now. Contact us to join the waitlist and we'll notify you when air duct cleaning launches.

How do I know if my air ducts need cleaning? +

Visible dust around vent covers, a musty smell when the HVAC turns on, and uneven airflow between rooms can all be signs it's worth having your ducts inspected.

Will duct cleaning help with allergies? +

Duct cleaning removes accumulated dust and debris from accessible components, but the EPA notes that duct cleaning has not been shown to prevent health problems. We don't make health or allergy-relief claims about this service.

How long does air duct cleaning take? +

A full home air duct cleaning typically takes 2–4 hours depending on the number of vents and system complexity.
