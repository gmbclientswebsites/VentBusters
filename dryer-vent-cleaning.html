Why It Matters

A Common, Often-Preventable Fire Risk

Lint is highly flammable, and it builds up inside your dryer vent with regular use. A clogged vent can trap heat, force your dryer to work harder, and contribute to one of the more common causes of home fires — regular cleaning can help reduce that risk.

Questions

Dryer Vent Cleaning FAQ

How often should I clean my dryer vent? +

The U.S. Fire Administration recommends cleaning your dryer vent at least once a year. Homes with long duct runs, pets, or daily laundry loads should be cleaned every 6–9 months.

What are the signs my dryer vent is clogged? +

Clothes taking multiple cycles to dry, a burning smell when the dryer runs, the dryer feeling hot to the touch, and visible lint around the exterior vent cover are all warning signs.

How long does dryer vent cleaning take? +

A standard dryer vent cleaning takes 30–45 minutes from start to finish.
