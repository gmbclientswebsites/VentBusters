Our Policy

Why there are only 3 of them

Because that is how many we have. Plenty of contractor sites open with a wall of five-star quotes and a rating in the header, and there is no way for you to tell from the page which of them are real. Everything above is published on Google under the reviewer’s own name, so you can go and check it against the source rather than taking our word for it.

We do not offer discounts or incentives in exchange for reviews, we do not edit or shorten what people write, and we will not put up a star rating we cannot show you the basis for. When there are more, they will appear here, including any that are not five stars.

If we have worked on your vent

A review helps more than you would think, and an honest three-star one helps more than a glowing one we had to ask twice for. You can leave one on our Google listing.

What we publish
Reviews from our Google Business Profile, verbatim
What we will not publish
Written-in testimonials, edited quotes, incentivised reviews
Review markup
None. Google does not support it for a business’s own reviews on its own site
What we do show
Airflow measured before and after, on every job

The thing worth judging us on is the work: how we clean a vent, what we do when a run turns out to be damaged rather than dirty, and where we stop and tell you it is not our job.

Judge Us on the Next Job Instead

Book a dryer vent cleaning and ask for the before-and-after airflow readings. That is the part that is hard to fake.
