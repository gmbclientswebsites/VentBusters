Questions

Inspection FAQ

Is the inspection really free? +

Yes. Every inspection is free and comes with zero obligation to book a cleaning. We'll walk you through exactly what we find and let you decide next steps.

What does the inspection check? +

We visually inspect your dryer vent path, exterior vent cap, and accessible ductwork, and run an airflow test to check for restrictions or blockages.

Do you offer same-day inspections? +

Yes, same-day and next-day inspection slots are often available.

Is this an official fire inspection? +

No. This is a visual inspection of accessible dryer-vent components and an airflow test performed by our technicians — it is not a government, code-compliance, or certified fire inspection.
