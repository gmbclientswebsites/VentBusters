What We Do · Boca Raton to Port St. Lucie

Dryer Vent & Air Duct Services

Vent Busters provides dryer vent cleaning, blockage removal, air duct cleaning and selected HVAC airflow services for homeowners throughout Palm Beach County and the Treasure Coast.

Our services

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Professional cleaning of the accessible dryer exhaust pathway to remove accumulated lint and debris.

  • Rotary brush and vacuum cleaning
  • Airflow check before and after
  • Exterior termination inspection when safely accessible
Learn About Dryer Vent Cleaning

Dryer Vent Blockage Removal

Heavy lint buildup and other obstructions can severely restrict dryer airflow. We locate and remove accessible blockages.

  • Heavy lint blockage removal
  • Accessible debris removal
  • Airflow check after clearing
Get a Blockage Removal Quote

Air Duct Cleaning

Professional cleaning of accessible supply and return ductwork to remove accumulated dust and debris.

  • Supply and return duct cleaning
  • Register and grille cleaning
  • Scope explained before work begins
Learn About Air Duct Cleaning

HVAC UV Light Systems

UV light systems are designed to treat specific areas inside compatible HVAC systems.

  • Product selected for your equipment
  • Installed per manufacturer requirements
  • Lamp replacement guidance
Ask About UV Light Options

HVAC System Sanitization

Sanitization may be available as an additional service after appropriate cleaning when the system and product label allow it.

  • Applied per manufacturer label
  • Offered after appropriate cleaning
  • Not mold assessment or remediation
Ask About System Sanitization
