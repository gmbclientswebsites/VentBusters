Duct Routing

In a Condo, the Dryer Is Rarely on an Outside Wall

That single fact drives most of what we run into here. In stacked laundry closets the duct turns almost immediately and then travels — up a chase toward a rooftop cap, or sideways to a sidewall termination that can sit two or three floors above grade. Lint does not settle evenly along a run like that. It packs at the elbows and at the last few feet before the outlet, which is exactly the stretch a shop vacuum at the wall never touches.

Our approach is to size rotating brushes to the actual duct diameter, work the run from both ends where the layout allows, and then confirm airflow at the termination instead of assuming the machine feels better. On the smaller single-family homes toward the inland streets, the story flips: shorter runs, but often older flex tucked behind the appliance that has been crushed against the wall for years.

Oceanfront Exposure

Salt Air Attacks the Outdoor Half of the System

The duct inside your wall may be fine while the part you never look at is the actual restriction.

  • Corroded hoods and caps on salt-exposed elevations
  • Dampers that stick part-open, or stop opening at all
  • Screens or mesh added over an outlet that should stay clear
  • Nesting material behind a flapper that no longer seats
  • Loose or unsealed joints at a rooftop transition
  • Airflow measured at the outlet, not guessed at the dryer

Juno Beach Questions

Access, Roofs and Salt

Do you clean dryer vents in Juno Beach condo buildings? +

Yes. In multi-unit buildings we work within whatever access the property allows. If your unit's duct feeds a shared chase or a common riser, the association or management company normally has to authorize work on that section, so tell us the building type when you call. We will explain what can be reached from inside the unit and what needs approval first.

My dryer duct goes up through the roof instead of out a wall. Can that still be cleaned? +

Often, yes. Rooftop terminations are common in Juno Beach condos and two-story homes, and lint tends to pack into the last stretch where the duct turns vertical. We clean the accessible pathway with rotating brushes sized to the duct and check airflow at the outlet afterward. If the roof cannot be reached safely, or building rules restrict roof access, we clean what is reachable from the interior and document the rest for you.

Does being this close to the ocean affect the vent hood or duct? +

Salt-laden air is hard on the outdoor half of the system. Hoods and dampers near the water can corrode, and a stiff or seized flapper restricts airflow even when the duct behind it is clear. We inspect the exterior termination on every visit and tell you plainly if the hood looks like it needs replacing rather than only cleaning.

Our laundry sits in an interior closet in a smaller single-family home. Does that take longer? +

Usually a little longer. When the dryer is not against an exterior wall the duct has to travel, and every elbow is a place lint settles. Interior laundry closets in older Juno Beach houses also hide crushed or kinked flex behind the appliance. We move the dryer where it is safe to do so, clean the run from both ends when the layout allows, and reconnect everything properly before we leave.
