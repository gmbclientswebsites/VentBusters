Why This Area Is Different

One Community, Several Building Eras

Closer to the county core you can often guess a home's duct layout from the name of the development, because whole streets went up the same year to the same plan. Hobe Sound does not read that way. It was built out in stages over decades across very different lots, so we cannot assume much before walking the property.

That variety shows up in the part that matters: how far the exhaust has to travel, how many elbows it turns through, and whether it leaves through a wall, a gable end or the roof. Two neighbors can need genuinely different work.

So we ask about the age of the house and any laundry-room remodels when you call, and we trace the run on arrival before a brush comes off the van.

Where the Duct Tends to Go

Four patterns we plan for around Hobe Sound, and what each one does to the lint.

🧱

Mid-Century Block Ranches

Short sidewall runs out through concrete block. Helpful in principle, except the flexible connector behind the appliance is often crushed against the wall.

🏘️

Later Subdivision Homes

Laundry parked beside the garage, with the duct crossing a ceiling or joist bay before it turns out at a gable end. Every turn is somewhere lint can stall.

🏠

Newer Two-Story Builds

Upstairs laundry feeding a vertical riser to a roof cap. What does not clear the top settles back down onto the first elbow above the dryer.

🌾

Rural and Manufactured Homes

Wider lots, longer approaches, and ducting that may run below the floor or through a skirted underside rather than inside a wall cavity.

The Coastal Side

Salt Air Works on the Hardware, Not the Lint

Homes on the eastern side of Hobe Sound, nearer the Intracoastal, bring a problem the inland properties rarely have. Lint inside the duct behaves the same everywhere; it is the exterior termination that suffers. Louvers pit, mounting screws bleed rust down the wall, and a damper hinge seizes so the flap sits part-open or drops shut and stays there.

A seized damper is worth catching, because a part-closed flap restricts exhaust as effectively as a wad of lint, and one stuck open invites insects and nesting material into the last stretch of duct. Where the hardware can be freed, that is part of the visit; where it cannot, we say the hood needs replacing rather than cleaning it and calling it done.

The Full Line-Up, Out to Hobe Sound

Most calls begin with dryer vent cleaning, which covers the accessible pathway from the appliance connection out to the termination. If loads have stopped drying altogether or the machine quits partway through a cycle, that points toward dryer vent blockage removal instead, where the obstruction is located and pulled out rather than brushed past.

On the HVAC side, air duct cleaning addresses the accessible supply and return components. In houses added onto over the years, and there are plenty here, the ductwork is a mix of original and later runs, and we work only what can be safely reached. For an ongoing measure inside the air handler, ask about HVAC UV light systems; we scope those conservatively and to the manufacturer's stated coverage.

HVAC system sanitization is applied per the product label once cleaning is finished. It is a treatment step, not mold assessment or remediation, and a suspected moisture problem in a wall or an air handler closet belongs with a specialist in that field.

Not satisfied? Tell us within 14 days and we'll re-clean the accessible components at no charge, or refund that service. Hobe Sound sits at the northern end of our service area map, which runs from Boca Raton up to Port St. Lucie.

Asked in Hobe Sound

Questions From Coastal, Subdivision and Acreage Homes

Our Hobe Sound house is a 1960s block ranch with what looks like the original dryer duct. Can it still be cleaned? +

Usually. Sidewall runs in those ranches tend to be short, which works in your favor, but the material matters more than the age. If we find a crushed flexible section behind the appliance, or a duct narrower than the dryer outlet feeding it, we clean what is accessible and then show you exactly what is there. Cleaning cannot widen an undersized duct, and we would rather you hear that from us than wonder why the drying times barely moved.

We are on acreage west of US-1 with a long driveway and a well. Does that change how you set up? +

Very little. Dryer vent work runs off ordinary household power and needs no water supply, so a rural parcel is not an obstacle. What helps us is knowing the approach in advance: a soft or narrow drive, a gate code, or dogs in the yard. Tell us when you book and we plan the stop with enough time in it, because southern Martin County properties are spread out and we schedule them accordingly.

Our home sits near the Intracoastal and the vent hood looks corroded. Will cleaning free up the damper? +

Sometimes. Salt-laden air is hard on hood louvers, hinge pins and mounting screws, and a damper that has stiffened will not swing fully open or fully closed. Where the hardware can be cleaned and worked loose, we do that as part of the visit. Where corrosion has gone past that point, we will say so plainly and suggest replacing the hood rather than cleaning a part that no longer moves.

Our newer two-story Hobe Sound home has the laundry upstairs and the vent exits through the roof. Is that a harder job? +

It is a longer one. A vertical riser to a roof cap gives lint more chances to settle, and anything that does not clear the top tends to fall back and pack the first elbow above the dryer. We work the run from the appliance end and clear the cap when the roof can be reached safely. Pitch, height and weather all factor into that, and we will tell you before we start if the cap cannot be worked that day.
