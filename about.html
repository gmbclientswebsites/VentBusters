Palm Beach County & the Treasure Coast

Dryer Vent Specialists, Without the Pressure

Vent Busters serves homeowners from Boca Raton to Port St. Lucie, and dryer vents are the only thing we do. A dryer that takes two cycles to finish a load, a blocked exhaust line, a duct that has come apart in the attic, a crushed connector behind the machine — that is our whole trade, and the goal on every visit is to explain the problem clearly and recommend only the work the system actually needs.

We used to offer air duct cleaning, UV light systems and HVAC sanitization alongside it. We stopped, deliberately. A company that does one thing properly is more useful to you than one that does five things adequately, and specialising means we have seen your particular laundry setup before. If what you need is HVAC work, we will tell you plainly that it is not us.

We believe homeowners should know what they are paying for. That means upfront pricing, clear service scope and documentation of the work whenever access allows.

What You Get

A Straight Answer, Not a Sales Pitch

Failure to clean was the leading factor in home clothes-dryer fires analysed by the U.S. Fire Administration. Most homeowners never find out there is a problem until the symptoms arrive in the laundry room, because you cannot see inside your own duct.

That is the whole reason Vent Busters exists: a technician shows up at your home, opens up the system, and shows you exactly what is in there — photos included. If it is clean, we tell you. If it is not, you get an honest, upfront quote before anyone touches a tool.

No four-hour arrival windows, no upsells, no scare tactics. Just a trained technician who treats your home like it's his own and leaves it safer than he found it.

Who Turns Up

Meet Jacob

Jacob is the name our customers keep using. He is the technician who turns up, traces the run, does the work and walks you through what he found — so the person who quotes the job is the person who carries it out. There is no sales visit followed by a different crew a week later.

You do not have to take our word for how that goes. Two of the three reviews on our Google listing name him, unprompted:

Highly recommend Jacob. We had another company scheduled to clean our dryer vent, who had to cancel, and Jacob fit us into his schedule same day. He was professional, efficient and fairly priced. We will definitely use him again!Nico’s Adventures · Google

Same-day availability is not something we promise in advance, and we would rather say that plainly than let you assume it is guaranteed. When the schedule allows it, it happens.

You will not find a list of certifications under his name here. Dryer vent cleaning is not a licensed trade in Florida, and we are not going to invent credentials to fill the space. What you get instead is someone who does this one job all day, shows you the airflow reading before and after, and tells you when nothing needs doing.
